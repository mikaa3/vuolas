How Vuolas Works

Vuolas (Finnish for swift-flowing, abundant current) visualizes live water flow across 42,578 Finnish river and lake segments using open data from the Finnish Environment Institute (SYKE). You don't need a hydrology background—here is what every control and term means in plain language.

1. Visual Encoding Modes

Percentile (p1–p99) Best for beginners

Compares each river to its own 15-year history rather than to other rivers. Why? Because 20 m³/s is a major flood in a small stream, yet unusually dry for a giant river like Kemijoki.

  • p10 (Dry / Low): Lower flow than 90% of historical days.
  • p50 (Normal / Median): Typical everyday water flow for that river.
  • p90+ (High / Flood): Wetter than 90% of days—top 10% highest flows.
Volume (m³/s — Discharge)

Shows the actual physical amount of water passing a point every second in cubic meters. 1 m³/s = 1,000 liters per second (roughly five full bathtubs passing every second). Thicker, brighter lines show Finland's biggest river trunks.

None (Plain Map)

Turns off all data-driven colors, outer glow, and thickness scaling so all rivers appear with clean, uniform lines—useful for studying river geography without visual clutter.

2. Top Summary Cards (National Overview)

Stations: Number of automated SYKE river stations reporting recent measurements across Finland.
Median Pct: The nationwide "middle" river condition right now (around p50 is average; p80+ means rivers nationwide are running high).
High (≥p85) & Low (≤p15): Count of stations currently experiencing unusually high water vs. dry conditions.

3. Map Layers & Navigation

Animate downstream flow: Streams glowing droplets strictly downstream from upland headwaters toward the sea. In Percentile and Volume modes, droplets move faster and glow wider on higher-volume rivers.
Show SYKE gauging stations: Toggles circular markers at the exact physical locations where sensors measure water levels and flow.
Show lake through-flow centerlines: Unlike steep mountain countries, Finnish rivers constantly pass through thousands of lakes. Virtual connector lines trace unbroken water paths across lake chains; uncheck this if you prefer to display only river channels.
Search & Major River Systems: Type any station, river basin, or municipality name to zoom straight to it, or use the quick-jump river buttons.

4. Clicking a River: Hydrograph & Key Terms

SYKE Gauging Station Measured directly at an official sensor station (or along its main river corridor).
Catchment-Scaled Reach Smaller tributaries don't all have sensors. Their flow is estimated from the nearest basin station's runoff (liters draining per km² of land per second) multiplied by the reach's upstream drainage area.
Reach Area (km² — Drainage / Catchment Basin): Total land and lake surface area uphill that drains rainwater and snowmelt into that specific river stretch.
7-Day Trend (%): Percentage change in flow compared to 7 days ago (+ means rising water, − means receding water).
30-Day Hydrograph Chart: The blue line plots the last 30 days of daily flow, the dashed horizontal line marks the 15-year normal median (p50), and the shaded band shows the normal p10–p90 range.

Vuolas runs 100% serverlessly with zero backend database by decoupling static river geometry on Cloudflare Pages from lightweight hourly JSON snapshots updated by GitHub Actions.

1. One-Time GIS Graph (scripts/build_network.py): Accumulates topological drainage area (km²) across 42,578 SYKE Uoma10 reaches, computes 15-year empirical percentiles (2012–2026), snaps gauges, and slices 2,110 static .pbf vector tiles.
2. Hourly Sync (update-flows.yml): Queries SYKE's OData v1.1 API every hour at :14 and force-pushes a single-commit orphan live-data branch (latest_flows.json ~90 KB + station_series.json ~251 KB) so Git history never bloats.
3. Live Runtime Join & 60 FPS Canvas: MapLibre joins live station runoff (l/s/km²) onto vector tiles in WebGL via Q = Area × Runoff / 1000, while a HiDPI Float32Array canvas animates downstream droplets.
